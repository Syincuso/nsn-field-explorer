# NSN Field Explorer: Privacy Policy

Effective 13 November 2026.

NSN Field Explorer is a browser extension for people who build and support NetSuite. It shows the field IDs, labels, types and values of the NetSuite record open in the browser. This policy describes what it does with that information.

## Summary
The extension does not collect, sell, share or send your data to anyone. It has no servers, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It asks for the fewest permissions possible: it can't see any website until you click its button, and it runs nothing in the background.

## What the extension reads
- The extension only runs when you click its toolbar button or press its keyboard shortcut (Alt+Shift+F). Until then it has no access to any page.
- When it runs, it reads the NetSuite record page open in that tab: field IDs, labels, types, values and sublist lines. These can include personal information that is in the record, such as names, email addresses and phone numbers. The extension only displays them to you.
- Some information is not on the page, so the extension asks **your own NetSuite account** for it, using NetSuite's own page modules and your existing NetSuite session:
  - `N/record` loads the saved record. It is used in view mode (where NetSuite puts few values on the page), for the Not on form tab, and for Compare with the saved record.
  - `N/search` looks up the names of list values that the page does not show.
  - `N/format` formats dates the way your NetSuite account does.
  - `N/query` looks up the definitions of custom fields and custom segments (internal ID, script ID and type) to link them to their setup pages.

  These requests are read-only. They go only to the NetSuite account you are signed in to, never to the developer or any third party.
- The extension never changes or saves anything in NetSuite.

## What the extension stores
Everything is stored in your browser's storage for your NetSuite site, and nothing leaves your computer:
- **Panel settings**, in `localStorage`, until you clear your browser data:
  - the panel's position, size and collapsed state;
  - column widths;
  - theme and compact view;
  - what a click copies;
  - whether View Raw shows JSON as a tree or as text.
- **A pinned record**, in `sessionStorage`, only if you click Pin, for the Compare tab. It is a snapshot of the record's field values and up to 500 lines per sublist, at most 2 MB. It is deleted when you close the browser tab, or when you click **Unpin** in the Pin menu.

The extension uses no cookies, no other browser storage and no extension sync storage.

## Copying and downloading
Record data goes to your clipboard or into a file only when you ask for it. That means clicking (or pressing Enter on) a field ID, a value, a title-bar item, a JSON tree item, Copy or Download, or clicking a field on the form while Inspect is on. Those copies and files are yours to manage.

## Links
The panel has these links:
- syincuso.com, the developer's website;
- NetSuite's Records Catalog;
- for custom fields, their setup pages in your own NetSuite account;
- on the help page, the GitHub pages of two open-source tools that inspired this one, and an email address.

They open only when you click them, like any link; the extension itself sends nothing. The links to the website carry a tag naming the extension and where in the panel the link was (for example `utm_content=help`), so the website can count visits from the extension. The tag identifies nothing about you or your NetSuite account.

## Data sharing
None. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party, and no data is sold or used for advertising or credit decisions.

## Permissions
- `activeTab`: lets the extension read the current tab only after you click its button or press the shortcut.
- `scripting`: lets it add its panel to that tab.

The extension asks for no other permissions, no access to any website in advance, and no access to your browsing history, bookmarks or other tabs.
